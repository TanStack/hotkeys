Recording Sequences

scope.createHotkeyRecorder with maxSteps records up to that many chords. Click Edit, press each chord in order, then press Enter (or reach four chords) to save. One chord saves as a regular hotkey. Escape cancels; Backspace removes the last chord or clears when empty.
