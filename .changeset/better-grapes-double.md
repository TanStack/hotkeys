Add Alpine, Ember, and Octane adapters for shortcuts, sequences, held keys, modifier hints, registration state, and recording. Use TanStack Store subscriptions with framework lifecycle cleanup, and share registration reconciliation through the core adapter entry point.

Support reactive scoped defaults in Alpine and Ember, provider context readers in Octane, and live recorder option getters so active recording sessions use current validation, callbacks, and configuration.
